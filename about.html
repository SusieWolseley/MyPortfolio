Transferable skills and soft skills

Teamwork

As part of my degree, I have completed multiple group projects. These involved teamwork with people I didn't know well, making sure every person is communicating and taking responsibility for my own work.

Strong problem solving skills

Aside from being a major part of why I love Computer Science, my enjoyment of problem solving has led me to play DnD. This has allowed me to develop these skills through coming up with solutions to the different obstacles we have to face while operating within the constraints of the world and our character's abilities.

Proactive approach to learning

I have spent over a year learning archery at my university's club. I have enjoyed working out how to improve my techniques by observing my current practices and working out how I can improve them, as well as asking for advice from more senior archers when I'm not sure where I'm going wrong. This has led to me to representing my university in an archery competition.
